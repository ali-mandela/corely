What's inside

Everything an SMB needs, nothing it doesn't.

🏪
Point of Sale
Create bills with line items, apply discounts, split payment modes (cash, UPI, card, credit), auto-deduct stock, hold & resume bills.
📦
Inventory Management
Stock movements, purchase entries, adjustments, real-time stock summary, and a full ledger view. Multi-store aware.
🧾
GST Invoicing
Auto-generate invoice numbers per financial year. CGST/SGST/IGST based on customer state. HSN-wise tax summary. PDF export.
🏬
Multi-store
Multiple locations with separate stock, managers, and POS sessions. Stock transfers between stores. Store-wise sales reports.
📊
Reports & Dashboard
Daily/weekly/monthly sales, top items, low stock alerts, vendor dues, customer balances, margin reports, CSV/Excel export.
🔐
RBAC & Audit Logs
4 roles: super admin, admin, manager, employee. Every create/update/delete is logged with before/after diffs. TTL-based retention.
👥
Customers & Vendors
Full CRUD for both. Link customers to sales, vendors to purchase entries. Groundwork for AP/AR outstanding tracking.
🏢
Multi-tenant Architecture
Each org gets slug-prefixed collections. Completely isolated data. One deployment, infinite orgs. Setup wizard with slug check.
🔄
Sale Returns & Quotations
Credit notes for returns, delivery challan generation, quotation/estimate workflows. Full document lifecycle out of the box.
